Subject: CNC Machining RFQ: RoMini Storybox 130mm Wooden Audio Cube

Dear CNC Team,

Please find attached our 1:1 vector CAD package for machining a 130mm cube prototype enclosure for our children's audio storybox.

Job Summary:
1. Materials:
   - Sheet 1: 6.0 mm BB/BB Grade Baltic Birch Plywood (Chassis walls & baseplate)
   - Sheet 2: 3.0 mm BB/BB Grade Baltic Birch Plywood (Top lid with 2.5D relief)

2. Joinery:
   - 10-segment finger joints (13mm tab x 6mm depth). Please apply R1.6mm corner dogbone fillets for your 1/8" (3.175mm) downcut bit to guarantee flush assembly.

3. Features:
   - Front Wall: 16x16 matrix of 2.8mm through-cut speaker holes.
   - Rear Wall: 22mm (USB-C) and 16mm (pushbutton) circular through-holes.
   - Top Lid (Sheet2_3mm_BalticBirch_TopLid.svg, units in millimetres):
       CUT = red stroke, through-cut the 130 mm rounded plate.
       SCORE = blue stroke, three rings, about 0.2 mm deep.
       ENGRAVE = black artwork, about 0.5 mm deep. Do not engrave a background.

All cut files are organized into those named CAM layers.
